Summary

This summary is part of the Privacy Policy. The full text follows it.

  • Who we are. SportsLink is operated by Truesight Solutions Private Limited, Pune, India. We decide how your personal data is used.
  • What we collect. Your email address, and the profile you build: name, photo, date of birth, gender, address, city, usual venue and sports. We also collect what you do in the App: challenges, matches, scores, ratings, reports, messages and follows.
  • What others see. Other players see your name, photo, age, gender, city, usual venue, approximate distance, reputation and results. They never see your email, date of birth, address or exact location.
  • Who we share it with. Only the service providers who run the App for us, and authorities where the law requires. We do not sell your data, we do not use third-party advertising, and we do not use tracking or analytics tools.
  • Your choices. You can edit your profile, switch notifications off, block other players, and delete your account in Settings at any time.
  • Questions or complaints. Write to support@sportslink.in.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

1.1 This Privacy Policy explains how Truesight Solutions Private Limited, CIN U72900PN2016PTC166153, Unit 307, SN 759/21+22, Deccan Gymkhana Lane No. 4, BDB Deodhar Road, Pune, Maharashtra 411004, India ("Truesight", "we", "us"), collects, uses, shares and protects personal data. It applies when you use the SportsLink mobile application (the "App") and the website at www.sportslink.in (the "Website"), together the "Service".

1.2 For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act"), Truesight is the Data Fiduciary for your personal data.

1.3 Contact for privacy questions and your rights: support@sportslink.in (subject line "Privacy").

1.4 This Privacy Policy is a separate notice. It is written to be read and understood on its own. Words not defined here have the meaning given in our Terms of Use.

2. The laws we follow

2.1 We process personal data in accordance with:

  • the Information Technology Act, 2000;
  • the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011;
  • the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021; and
  • the DPDP Act and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, as their provisions come into force.

3. The personal data we collect

3.1 Account.

  • Your email address, used to create your account and send your sign-in codes.
  • The date, time and version of the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy you accepted.

3.2 Profile. You provide these when you set up or edit your profile:

  • your first and last name;
  • a profile photo (optional);
  • your date of birth (required, to confirm you are 18 or older);
  • your gender;
  • your home address;
  • your city;
  • your usual venue (optional); and
  • the sports you play.

When you choose your address from the suggestions shown, we also store the map coordinates and place reference that Google returns for it.

3.3 Activity in the App. We collect:

  • the challenges you send, receive and respond to, including proposed dates, times and venues;
  • your matches, the scores you submit, and the results, points, rankings, skill ratings and reputation score that follow;
  • the star ratings you give and receive;
  • the reports you make;
  • the messages you send in challenge chats and team chats;
  • the players and teams you follow;
  • the "cheers" you give;
  • the players you block; and
  • your teams, team roles and lineups.

3.4 Device and technical data.

  • A push-notification token for your device, if you allow notifications.
  • The time you last opened the App.
  • Your notification settings.
  • Technical logs created when your device connects to our servers, such as IP address, device and browser type, and the time of each request. These are used for security and to fix faults.

3.5 From other people.

  • Other players may submit information about you: match scores, star ratings, reports, and team lineups that include you.
  • A Team Admin may invite you to a team.

3.6 Support. Anything you tell us when you contact us.

3.7 What we do not collect.

  • We do not access your device's GPS location, contacts, camera, microphone or files. The only exception is a photo you choose to upload.
  • We do not collect payment card, bank or UPI details.
  • We do not collect health, biometric or financial information.
  • The App contains no advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking software.

4. Why we use your data, and our legal basis

4.1 We use your personal data only for these purposes:

  • Running your account: creating it, signing you in, and keeping it secure.
  • Providing the Service: showing your profile to other players, matching you with players and teams by sport, city, gender and division, and sending and managing challenges. Also: recording scores, calculating points, rankings, skill ratings and reputation, running teams and lineups, and delivering messages.
  • Location features: turning your address into a map location, so we can show your city's players and the approximate distance between players. Suggesting venues near you.
  • Notifications: telling you about challenges, matches, messages, teams and reminders, and sending legal and safety notices.
  • Safety and integrity: enforcing our Terms of Use and Community Standards, reviewing reports and uploaded photos, preventing fake accounts, cheating, harassment and abuse, enforcing blocks, and suspending accounts.
  • Legal compliance: meeting our obligations under Indian law, responding to lawful requests from courts and authorities, and keeping the records the law requires.
  • Support and improvement: answering your questions, fixing faults, and improving the Service.

4.2 Legal basis. We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you create your account, and when you give it for any optional feature. We also rely on other lawful grounds where the DPDP Act allows. These include processing data you voluntarily give us for a specified purpose, complying with law or a court order, and responding to a medical emergency or a threat to anyone's safety.

4.3 We do not use your personal data for advertising, and we do not make decisions about you that produce legal effects solely by automated means. Rankings, skill ratings and reputation scores are calculated automatically from match results, ratings and upheld reports, as explained in the App.

5. What other people can see

5.1 Other signed-in players can see:

  • your name, photo, gender, city and usual venue;
  • your age in years (not your date of birth);
  • your approximate distance from them, in whole kilometres;
  • your reputation score, the number of ratings you have received, and your sports;
  • the players and teams you follow, and who follows you;
  • your accepted challenges and your matches, results and cheers;
  • your leaderboard positions and skill ratings; and
  • your team memberships.

5.2 Photos and team logos are stored in publicly readable storage. Anyone who has the web address of the image file can view it.

5.3 Messages in a challenge chat are visible to the players (or team members) in that challenge. Team chat is visible to the team's members.

5.4 Only you can see: your email address, date of birth, home address, map coordinates, notification settings, and the list of players you have blocked.

5.5 Ratings are blind: you see a rating another player gave you only after you have rated them.

5.6 Reports are seen only by Truesight's moderators. The player you report is not told who reported them.

5.7 Truesight's authorised moderators can access your account details, profile, messages, reports and uploaded photos. They do so only to review reports and photos, enforce our Terms of Use, keep players safe, provide support, and comply with law.

6. Who we share your data with

6.1 We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share it only with the following people and organisations.

6.2 Service providers (Data Processors) who process data on our instructions to run the Service:

  • Supabase, Inc. hosts our database, sign-in system, file storage, real-time chat and server functions, on Amazon Web Services infrastructure in India (Mumbai).
  • Google LLC (Google Maps Platform). When you search for an address or venue, the text you type is sent to Google through our servers. So are a reference location to rank nearby results (your city centre, or for venue searches your home location) and the place you select. Your use of these features is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Google LLC (Google Workspace) delivers our emails, including your sign-in codes.
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc.), Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver push notifications to your device. A notification contains its text, which may include a player's or team's name, a venue and time, or a short preview of a chat message.
  • GitHub, Inc. hosts the Website. The Website also loads fonts from Google Fonts and scripts from unpkg.com, which receive your IP address when you visit.

6.3 We require our service providers to protect your personal data with safeguards at least as protective as those described in this Privacy Policy, and to use it only to provide their services to us.

6.4 Other players, as described in Section 5.

6.5 Legal and safety disclosures. We may disclose personal data to courts, law enforcement and government agencies where Indian law requires or permits it. That includes information requests under the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the reporting of child sexual abuse material under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012. We may also disclose it where necessary to protect the life, safety or rights of any person.

6.6 Business transfers. If Truesight is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring or sale of assets, personal data may be transferred to the successor. It will remain protected in line with this Privacy Policy.

7. Storage and transfers outside India

7.1 Your account and profile data are stored in India. Some of our service providers (Section 6.2) may process data in other countries, including the United States, for example to deliver emails and push notifications. We transfer personal data outside India only as permitted by the DPDP Act and any restrictions the Government of India notifies under it.

8. How long we keep your data

8.1 We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes in Section 4, or as the law requires:

  • Account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Map coordinates from Google: deleted automatically after 30 days (the App refreshes them while your account is active).
  • In-app notifications: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Challenge chat messages: deleted automatically 180 days after the challenge closes. A challenge closes when its match result is confirmed or recorded as unresolved, or when it is declined, withdrawn, expired or cancelled. A match that never gets a result counts as closed at its scheduled time. Team chat is kept while the team exists.
  • Matches, results, scores, rankings, skill ratings, star ratings and reports: kept for the life of the Service, because they form part of other players' records and the integrity of the leaderboards.
  • Registration data after account deletion: under Rule 3(1)(h) of the Intermediary Rules, we keep your email address, name, date of birth, gender, city and registration dates for 180 days after you delete your account, in restricted storage. They are then erased.
  • Record of your consent: the date, time and version of the documents you accepted, kept for 3 years after you delete your account, so we can show that consent was given.
  • Content removed by our moderators: kept for 180 days (Rule 3(1)(g) of the Intermediary Rules), then erased.
  • Security and technical logs: kept for as long as the law requires, which may be up to 1 year or longer if an authority directs.

8.2 Where a court or authority lawfully requires us to preserve data for longer, we will.

9. Deleting your account

9.1 In the App: open Settings → Delete account and confirm. Deletion takes effect immediately.

9.2 Without the App: email support@sportslink.in from the address registered to your account, with the subject line "Delete my account". We will verify the request and complete it within 30 days.

9.3 What happens when you delete your account:

  • Your profile photo, date of birth, gender, address, city, location, usual venue and notification settings are erased. Your name is replaced with "Deleted player".
  • Your follows, cheers, blocks, notifications, preferred sports and push tokens are deleted.
  • Your open challenges are withdrawn, booked matches that have not been played are cancelled, and you leave your teams.
  • You are signed out everywhere, and the account cannot be used again.
  • Matches, results, rankings, ratings and reports you took part in remain on other players' records, attributed to "Deleted player". Your challenge chat messages also remain, until they are deleted on the schedule in Section 8.
  • Your registration data and consent record are kept for the periods in Section 8 and then erased.

9.4 If you hold a paid subscription (when available), deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

10. Your rights

10.1 Subject to Indian law, you have the right to:

  • Access: a summary of the personal data we process about you, the processing activities, and the identities of those we have shared it with.
  • Correction and completion: correct inaccurate or misleading data, and complete or update it. You can edit most profile details yourself in the App.
  • Erasure: have your personal data erased when it is no longer needed, unless we must keep it by law (see Sections 8 and 9).
  • Withdraw consent at any time (Section 11).
  • Grievance redressal: complain to us at support@sportslink.in and receive a response.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapable.

10.2 How to exercise your rights. Email support@sportslink.in from your registered email address, with the subject line "Privacy request", and say which right you wish to exercise. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will respond within 30 days at most.

10.3 Complaints. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the DPDP Act, once it is accepting complaints. For content-related grievances, you may appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee at https://gac.gov.in.

10.4 You must not register a false or frivolous complaint or give false information when exercising these rights (Section 15 of the DPDP Act).

11. Consent and how to withdraw it

11.1 You give consent by ticking the agreement box when you create an account, after reading this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use to the end.

11.2 You can withdraw consent as easily as you gave it:

  • switch off optional notification categories in Settings;
  • turn off notifications for SportsLink in your device settings;
  • remove your photo or optional details in Edit profile; or
  • delete your account.

11.3 Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew. The Service cannot work without the core data in Section 3.1 and 3.2, so withdrawing consent for that data means deleting your account.

12. Notifications and emails

12.1 We send push notifications, in-app notifications and emails about your account and activity. You can switch off categories of notifications in Settings, and turn off push notifications entirely in your device settings.

12.2 Legal notices, such as changes to our Terms of Use or this Privacy Policy, and our periodic reminders, are always sent while you hold an account.

12.3 Notification previews may appear on your lock screen. Use your device's settings if you want to hide them.

13. How we protect your data

13.1 We use reasonable security practices and procedures appropriate to the nature of the data, including:

  • encryption of data in transit (TLS);
  • database access rules that let each player read and change only what they are allowed to;
  • keeping private profile fields out of other players' reach;
  • keeping third-party service keys on our servers, never in the App;
  • signing in by one-time email codes instead of passwords;
  • limiting staff access to those who need it; and
  • keeping security logs.

13.2 No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law, and tell you what happened and what you can do. Please keep the email account you use for SportsLink secure.

14. Children

14.1 SportsLink is only for people aged 18 and over. We ask for your date of birth and do not let anyone under 18 finish setting up an account. We do not knowingly process the personal data of children.

14.2 If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will suspend it and erase the personal data, except where the law requires us to keep it. If you believe a child is using SportsLink, please email support@sportslink.in.

15. Links and third-party services

15.1 The Service links to third-party services, such as Google Maps, that have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.

16. Changes to this Privacy Policy

16.1 We may update this Privacy Policy. We will tell you about material changes in the App, by push notification or by email before they take effect, and update the version and date above. If a change means using your personal data for a new purpose that needs your consent, we will ask for it.

17. Language

17.1 This Privacy Policy is written in English. On request, we will provide it in any language listed in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution of India.

18. Contact and complaints

18.1 For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email support@sportslink.in.

18.2 We will acknowledge your complaint within 24 hours and resolve it within 7 days of receipt.
