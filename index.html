The short version

Every game you play should count for something.

You already play every week. Somewhere in all of it there is a real record — the wins, the losses, the opponents who are genuinely better than you. Nothing keeps it. SportsLink does.

Challenge anyone in your sport. Agree a venue and a time. Play. Then you both report the score without seeing the other's — so the result is settled by the two people who were actually there, and by nobody else.

Confirmed results move your points, your skill rating and your reputation. Ten sports, thirty-eight formats, every scoring rule built in.

Challenge anyone

No fixture secretary, no season to wait for. Pick an opponent at your level and agree the terms yourselves.

Scores nobody can cook

Blind double-entry is enforced by the platform. A result only stands when both sides independently said the same thing.

Three ways to rank

Points for the table, an Elo rating for real strength, and a reputation score for whether you turn up and play fair.

Bring a team

Form a side, set a kit, pick a lineup and challenge other teams — same flow, same guarantees.

Amateur sport, properly scored

The loop

Four steps, then it's on the table.

Everything in SportsLink runs through the same cycle. No organisers, no fixtures list, no waiting for a season to start.

1

Challenge

Swipe through players in your sport and area. Send up to two proposals at once — venue, date and time.

2

Agree

They accept, counter, or let it lapse. Two counters a side, then the clock decides.

3

Play

Both sides confirm on match day. Kits are picked in advance so nobody turns up in clashing colours.

4

Report

You each submit the score without seeing the other's. Agreement confirms it and moves the table.

Challenges

One card at a time.

Open challenges arrive as a deck. Drag right to open a proposal, left to move on — or use the buttons. Every card carries the record that matters: played, won, drawn, lost.

  • Only open challenges enter the deck — countered and accepted ones never appear.
  • Switch identity to challenge as yourself or as any team you administer.
  • Counters are capped at two a side, so a negotiation cannot run forever.

Teams

Play as a side, score as one.

Any player can start a team. Admins set the kit, pick the lineup and act on the team's behalf — the challenge flow is the same one you already know.

The team takes the points

A win pays the team's row, not the eleven individual rows. Team results never distort a player's own record.

Players take a turn-out point

Everyone in the lineup earns a flat +1 for showing up — win, lose or draw. No individual W/D/L from a team match.

Skill rating still moves

A team match shifts each player's own rating against the opposing lineup's mean, frozen before anything updates. A different ladder, not a contradiction.

Catalog

Ten sports. Thirty-eight formats.

A sport is a row in a table, not a branch in the code. Its formats, scoring mode and points all come from that row — so adding one never ships an app update.

Under the hood

The parts that keep it honest.

Skill rating

An Elo ladder separate from points. Everyone starts at 1000, K falls as you play, and you stay provisional until five games are in.

Reputation

Rate each other after the match; ratings stay private until both sides have submitted. Upheld conduct reports cost ten points each.

Notifications

Thirty-two events, seven categories you control. Quiet hours hold anything non-urgent overnight.

Calendar

Every confirmed fixture, unconfirmed proposal and played match on one month grid, filterable down to a single day.

Your data

What we show, and what we never will.

A competitive record only works if people are willing to put their real results on it. That means being exact about what other players can see — and what stays yours.

Other players see
  • Your name and profile photo
  • Your city, preferred sports and usual venue
  • Your results, standings and skill rating
  • Your reputation score
  • How far away you are, in whole kilometres
They never see
  • Your date of birth
  • Your home address or exact location
  • Your email address
  • Your score until they have submitted theirs
  • Any report you file — those go to moderators only

Blind by design

Your score is withheld by the server, not by the app being polite. There is no request that returns your opponent's number before they have entered it.

Ratings stay sealed

The stars you give an opponent stay private until you have both rated each other, so nobody can trade a good rating for a good one back.

Chat is temporary

Messages exist only while a challenge is open, and notifications are cleared after thirty days. Neither sticks around once it has done its job.

Leaving is clean

Delete your account in Settings and your personal details are erased. Past matches stay on your opponents' records, credited to "Deleted player".

SportsLink does not sell your data. It is handled by the providers that run the service — the database and sign-in, push notifications, and turning your address into an approximate map location. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
