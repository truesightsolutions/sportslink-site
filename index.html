Questions

Who books the venue?

You and your opponent. Agree the venue and how to split the cost in the challenge chat.

What if our scores don't match?

You both get one more try. Still different? The match counts as played, with no winner.

Can I play as a team?

Yes. Create a team, invite players and take on other teams. Team admins send challenges and pick lineups.

Which sports can I play?

Badminton, tennis, pickleball, squash, table tennis, football, cricket, basketball, volleyball and American football, each in its own formats, like singles, doubles or 5-a-side.
